Gastón's Sweets — Commercial Management System

A confectionery business selling perishable stock had no way to know what was about to expire, which credit was overdue, or whether the day actually closed in profit. It ran on spreadsheets.

The problem

Sales, credit, purchasing and perishable stock tracked separately. No expiry control, no traceability, and cash closing done by hand every night.

What I built

A full management platform: sales with credit and payments, purchasing, FEFO inventory with lot and expiry tracking, daily cash, finance, ABC analytics, alerts and reporting.

The result

One auditable system covering purchase-to-cash. The dashboard scores operational health and points to exactly where money is trapped.

ZentrixKG — Business ERP & CRM Platform

A technology company ran sales, purchasing, inventory, service orders and warranties across disconnected spreadsheets — no single view of the business, and no record of who changed what.

The problem

Every department kept its own file. Quotes lived in one place, stock in another, service orders on paper. Nothing reconciled and nothing was auditable.

What I built

A complete ERP/CRM from scratch: CRM and quoting, purchasing, suppliers, inventory, expenses, service orders, technician management, warranties, analytics and per-module permissions.

The result

The full quote-to-warranty cycle in one auditable system, with CSRF protection, rate limiting, audit logging and automated deployment.

Descartables Mabel — Business Management Panel

A distributor needed a complete management panel that runs anywhere — no framework, no build step, no server. So I built one in plain JavaScript, and it still does real analytics.

The problem

Off-the-shelf systems needed servers, subscriptions and IT support the business did not have. Anything built had to run from a folder.

What I built

A modular single-page system: point of sale, sales, purchasing, inventory, clients, daily cash, reporting and an analytics dashboard — independent view modules over a dedicated state store.

The result

Zero external dependencies and zero build step, yet it surfaces churn risk, low-margin products, dead stock and overdue receivables automatically.
